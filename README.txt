ANCIENT REALM - digital adaptation (fan-made)
Open index.html in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). No server or internet needed.
On a phone: copy the whole folder to the device (or serve it from any static host) and open index.html.

Controls
- Drag a Market card onto your realm. A ghost shows the landing spot (green = legal, red = illegal).
- Blocks the card will cover glow: tap them to activate, then press Confirm. Then tap a deck to draw.
- Tap a checked block (green check) to un-select it. Reset turn (clock arrow) puts everything back to the start of the turn.
- Tap or long-press any card to enlarge it. Keys: Enter = confirm, Ctrl+Z = reset turn.

Folder: index.html, style.css, data.js (card data), game.js (rules + UI), assets/ (cards at 300 DPI for play, 600 DPI for zoom, icons, original rulebook panels).

Rules interpretations worth checking against your copy of the game
- "Adjacent" mine scores: Mine (2 per wonder adjacent) is read as adjacent to that mine, like the Collector's mine (4 per wonder adjacent to this mine). Monuments mine (3 per monument adjacent to a wonder) counts monuments touching any wonder.
- "Per citizen in one group" (Temple mine) uses your larger group (crown or commoner); Mausoleum mine scores 5 if either group has 3+.
- Wonder cost modifiers (Ambition, New Plans) ask which resource to add/remove.
- Placing: a card may overhang the realm edge while covering blocks; it may never cover any part of a wonder.
